# Main-text Potts proof map

This document maps Sections 2 and 3 of `main.tex`, including the imported files `sections/strict-ci-tools.tex` and `sections/strict-vigoda-ci.tex`, to the Lean proofs. It covers the model, concrete Vigoda coupling, complex-analytic transfer, both endpoints, and the Potts main theorem. The seven completed Appendix Potts regions have a separate [proof map and module guide](appendix/README.md). Holant is documented separately in [proof overview](overview.md) and [Holant review](holant.md). Lean paths below are relative to the repository root; paper labels and line numbers refer to the historical paper snapshot recorded in [the source manifest](provenance/source-manifest.json).

The verification environment is Lean 4.33.1 and mathlib v4.33.1.
`bash scripts/check-all.sh` builds the complete library and audits every
imported project declaration. Only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and
`Quot.sound` are allowed. The cited results are proved in the library, so
no public statement below takes a literature hypothesis. The [verification manifest](verification.json)
records the checked source closure, source hashes and build results.

The main-text independent-colour-field Lee–Yang proof is mapped separately in [Lee–Yang proof map](lee-yang.md), including all three vertex regimes, the edge-colouring corollary, and arbitrary pinning semantics.

The final theorems assert `∃ ε > 0, UniformPottsZeroFree C Δ ε`, placing the radius before the quantifiers over finite vertex types, graphs, and pinnings. It gives nonvanishing of the normalized partition function, identifies the only possible zeros of the full partition function in this neighborhood as the forced zero at `z = 0`, and gives its exact multiplicity. Thus the theorem chooses a radius for the entire graph family. The earlier fixed-instance result in `CI2ZF/Potts/Transfer/FixedGraph.lean` is an auxiliary theorem with a different quantifier order.

## Cited results and scope boundaries

- **Strict-regime CI is proved within the library.** `hardStep_W_drift_le` in `CI2ZF/Coupling/Vigoda/HardCouplingCost.lean` connects actual graph components and probability allocations. `PottsCITheorem` applies common-coin averaging, path coupling, and stationary comparison. The final strict theorem does not ask for a `ConditionalHardCouplingEstimate`.
- **Hard-coloring CI at equality is proved by a different route.** The paper cites CFFGZZ2024, Theorem 20 and Proposition 22. Lean does not formalize that proof. It extends the Carlson–Vigoda contraction to `q ≥ 11Δ/6` for `Δ ≥ 6`, which covers every integer point of the critical line, and proves both the internal `CriticalHardColouringInput` (`critical_hard_colouring_input`) and the original-graph `ExternalCriticalHardColouringTheorem` (`external_critical_hard_colouring_theorem`). As in the paper's cited route, the original-graph statement is also proved to imply the boundary-count version. Positive-temperature CI at equality is proved by the concrete Vigoda chain. See [cited results](external-inputs.md).
- **General graph-class transfer is a conditional theorem in the paper.** `GraphClassTransferInputs` and `TransferCouplingInputs` express its CI hypotheses. The transfer, the choice of a uniform radius, and the concrete model connections are proved in Lean. Closure of `AmbientRealization` under restriction keeps recursive instances within appropriate induced subgraphs and pinnings.
- **Arbitrary pinning includes improper pinning.** The normalized polynomial omits pinned-only edges and imposes no properness condition on the original pinning. The full polynomial's forced factor and exact zero multiplicity are proved separately. Conflicts created by adding a root pin are handled in the actual recursion.
- **Empty cases and endpoints are included.** With no free vertices the normalized partition function is 1. Wasserstein distance on an empty remaining vertex space is zero, and the empty hard chain holds. Empty exteriors, disconnected components, and single-vertex recursions are not excluded by hidden nonemptiness assumptions. The hard endpoint uses feasibility and finite-law limits; `x = 1` uses identities for the uniform law.
- **The concrete profile is distinguished from the general tools.** The implemented profile is `p_1=1, p_2=13/42, p_3=1/6, p_4=2/21, p_5=1/21, p_6=1/84`, and `p_s=0` for `s≥7`. All generic profile-ρ variants, ancillary irreducibility results, and unused variants are outside this map's completion claim. This implementation choice is not an unproved input to the completed main theorem.
- **All seven Appendix Potts regions are complete.** Their organized implementations and the cited results they use, all proved in Lean, are documented in [Appendix guide](appendix/README.md) and [STATUS.md](appendix/STATUS.md). `scripts/check-all.sh` verifies the full library.

## Verification and suggested reading order

Start with the statements in `CI2ZF/Potts/Theorems/PottsExternalTheorem.lean` and `CI2ZF/Potts/Theorems/PottsMainTheorem.lean`. The coupling branch runs through `TransferCouplingInputs → PottsCITheorem → HardCouplingCost`; the analytic branch runs through `UniformZeroFreePackaging → UniformInduction → Positive/HardUniformTransfer`. For general classes, read `GraphClassInputs → GraphClassCoupling → FamilyUniformTransfer → GraphClassPottsTransfer`. The principal concrete-model bridges are in `PottsModel`, `RootChildren`, `RootGibbsSemantics`, `GenericGibbsRelabel`, and `OptionBFSMarginals`.

From the repository root, `bash scripts/check-all.sh` builds the complete aggregate and runs its axiom audit. Individual entry points can be checked with `./scripts/lake.sh build CI2ZF.Potts.Theorems.PottsMainTheorem`, `CI2ZF.Potts.Theorems.GraphClassPottsTransfer`, or `CI2ZF.Potts.Theorems.PottsExternalTheorem`. For individual declarations, use `#print axioms` and inspect the explicit mathematical hypotheses as well: a theorem depending only on the three allowed logical axioms can still have explicit mathematical premises.
