# Main-text Potts proof map

This document maps Sections 2 to 4 of `main.tex` to the Lean proofs. It covers the model, the complex-analytic transfer at both endpoints, the soft flip kernels and the concrete Vigoda coupling, and the Potts main theorem. Statement numbers follow the current paper; the [coverage table](coverage.json) lists every numbered statement with its status and Lean names. The seven completed Appendix Potts regions have a separate [proof map and module guide](appendix/README.md). Holant is documented separately in [proof overview](overview.md) and [Holant review](holant.md). Lean paths below are relative to the repository root. Paper labels are those of the current sources; line numbers such as `main.tex:459` refer to the historical paper snapshot recorded in [the source manifest](provenance/source-manifest.json).

The verification environment is Lean 4.33.1 and mathlib v4.33.1.
`bash scripts/check-all.sh` builds the complete library and audits every
imported project declaration. Only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and
`Quot.sound` are allowed. The standalone Lean statements for the cited
ingredients tracked in [external-inputs.md](external-inputs.md) are proved
in the library, so no public statement below takes a literature hypothesis. The [verification manifest](verification.json)
records the checked source closure, source hashes and build results.

The main-text independent-colour-field Lee–Yang proof is mapped separately in [Lee–Yang proof map](lee-yang.md), including all three vertex regimes, the edge-colouring corollary, and arbitrary pinning semantics.

The final theorems assert `∃ ε > 0, UniformPottsZeroFree C Δ ε`, placing the radius before the quantifiers over finite vertex types, graphs, and pinnings. It gives nonvanishing of the normalized partition function, identifies the only possible zeros of the full partition function in this neighborhood as the forced zero at `z = 0`, and gives its exact multiplicity. Thus the theorem chooses a radius for the entire graph family. The earlier fixed-instance result in `ZeroFreeness/Potts/Transfer/FixedGraph.lean` is an auxiliary theorem with a different quantifier order.

## Cited results and scope boundaries

- **Strict-regime CI is proved within the library.** `hardStep_W_drift_le` in `ZeroFreeness/Coupling/Vigoda/HardCouplingCost.lean` connects actual graph components and probability allocations. `PottsCITheorem` applies common-coin averaging, path coupling, and stationary comparison. The final strict theorem does not ask for a `ConditionalHardCouplingEstimate`.
- **Hard-coloring CI at equality is proved by a different route.** The paper cites CFFGZZ2024, Theorem 20 and Proposition 22. Lean does not formalize that proof. The claim of companion Remark 4.4 that those results apply along the whole critical line is a statement about the cited paper and is not formalized. Lean instead extends the Carlson–Vigoda contraction to `q ≥ 11Δ/6` for `Δ ≥ 6`, which covers every integer point of the critical line, and proves both the internal `CriticalHardColouringInput` (`critical_hard_colouring_input`) and the original-graph `ExternalCriticalHardColouringTheorem` (`external_critical_hard_colouring_theorem`). As in the paper's cited route, the original-graph statement is also proved to imply the boundary-count version. Positive-temperature CI at equality is proved by the concrete Vigoda chain. See [cited results](external-inputs.md).
- **General graph-class transfer is a conditional theorem in the paper.** `GraphClassTransferInputs` and `TransferCouplingInputs` express its CI hypotheses. The transfer, the choice of a uniform radius, and the concrete model connections are proved in Lean. Closure of `AmbientRealization` under restriction keeps recursive instances within appropriate induced subgraphs and pinnings.
- **Arbitrary pinning includes improper pinning.** The normalized polynomial omits pinned-only edges and imposes no properness condition on the original pinning. The full polynomial's forced factor and exact zero multiplicity are proved separately. Conflicts created by adding a root pin are handled in the actual recursion.
- **Empty cases and endpoints are included.** With no free vertices the normalized partition function is 1. Wasserstein distance on an empty remaining vertex space is zero, and the empty hard chain holds. Empty exteriors, disconnected components, and single-vertex recursions are not excluded by hidden nonemptiness assumptions. The hard endpoint uses feasibility and finite-law limits; `x = 1` uses identities for the uniform law.
- **General profiles and the concrete profile.** `SoftFlipKernel.lean` proves the profile tools of Section 4 for every profile ρ, with `R_ρ < ∞` where the paper assumes it, and every `x ∈ (0,1]`, including irreducibility when `ρ₁ > 0`. The main proof uses the Vigoda profile `p_1=1, p_2=13/42, p_3=1/6, p_4=2/21, p_5=1/21, p_6=1/84`, and `p_s=0` for `s≥7` (`vigodaProfile`); `cvProfile` is the Carlson–Vigoda profile of the companion.
- **Two unnumbered claims in the text are proved.** `ZeroFreeness/Potts/Theorems/ThresholdComparison.lean` proves the comparison after Theorem 1.1: for integer `q`, the condition `q ≥ 11Δ/6` is strictly weaker than the earlier rows of the threshold table for `Δ = 2` and every `Δ ≥ 6` (`newCond_strictly_weaker`), and coincides with `q ≥ 2Δ` for `3 ≤ Δ ≤ 5` (`newCond_iff_lss_of_small`). `ZeroFreeness/Potts/Model/PinnedMarginal.lean` proves the remark after Theorem 1.2: a colour on `k ≥ 1` pinned neighbours of a free vertex has conditional marginal of order `x^k` (`marginal_order_pow`, `marginal_not_bounded_below`).
- **All seven Appendix Potts regions are complete.** Their organized implementations and the formalized versions of the cited ingredients they use are documented in [Appendix guide](appendix/README.md) and [STATUS.md](appendix/STATUS.md). `scripts/check-all.sh` verifies the full library.
